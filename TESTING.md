# Testing Little Monkey

## Automated tests

Run these in the project folder before every commit:

```
npm test
```

They start the game in a pretend browser (no window opens) and check that it loads without errors and shows the title screen, for a new player and for a saved game. They catch typos, missing files and files loaded in the wrong order.

They also check the content files: every word in a sentence is in the word bank, no word is listed twice, every picture is different, characters stand where you can walk, names have Chinese, pinyin and English, the word pictures are complete and up to date, and so on. If a test fails, its message says what's wrong and where.

One test plays Flower Fruit Mountain from a new game to the end of what's built (chapter 1: the school, its lessons and its flowers), pressing the game's own buttons the way a player would (tests/playthrough.test.js). If a change makes the area impossible to finish, it says where it got stuck.

And they try out a few things the game does: the story questions only show words you've caught, it's chapter 1 all the way (no prologue), and Space and Enter work as A on the title screen and in the intro.

They can't tell whether the game *feels* right, so the checks below are still needed.

## Playing on this computer

```
npm start
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in a browser. Press Ctrl+C in the terminal to stop. In Claude Code, the preview pane's "game" setting does the same.

## The screens gallery

With `npm start` running, open http://127.0.0.1:8000/tools/screens.html. It shows every screen of the game side by side at phone size (the title, the opening, tips, How to play, conversations, replies, word cards, fill the gap, the shop, animals, the menu pages, the story and the cave door), each the real game with its own pretend save. Your saved game isn't touched, and it's silent. Change the size to check a small phone or a computer, and "alone" shows one screen bigger. The smallest, Small phone (640 × 360), has its own tighter layout: check that the word test shows both rows, the Word Scroll a whole row of tiles, Settings all its buttons and a battle its answers and the apple without scrolling, a battle where you're hurt and have fruit its three choices (Hit, Eat, Run away) and your hearts, and the bag its Back. At iPhone (750 × 369) and smaller, every card fits but the Tasks page, the scrolls, How to play and Add to Home Screen, which show ▼ More; no A or B badge covers a letter (OK, Next, Skip, New game, Done, Music); pinyin is never tiny; and in conversations you stay in sight above or below the speech box. To see them as a phone does, open the gallery in a browser at 3× zoom (a phone's pixels): pictures are sized for the screen's real pixels, so a computer's gallery shows some a little bigger or smaller than a phone.

After any change you can see, look through it for things that overlap, buttons you'd have to scroll to find, text that's too long, and screens that don't match the others. A red "Error" under a screen means it didn't load. The screens are listed in tools/screens.js; add one when the game gets a new screen.

## Playing on your phone

Open https://lph147.github.io/little-monkey/ on your phone. GitHub Pages updates it a minute or two after each upload to GitHub. Your phone may keep showing the previous version for up to 10 minutes; if a change hasn't appeared, wait a little and reload.

The save on your phone belongs to this address, so it is separate from saves anywhere else.

### Installing it as an app (full screen)

- **Android (Chrome):** open the address, then menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). Open 小猴子 from the home screen: no address bar, status bar or navigation buttons, and it stays sideways even when the phone is upright. Your save is the same one as in Chrome.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address, then Share → **Add to Home Screen** → Add. Open 小猴子 from the home screen and turn the phone sideways: no Safari bars and no status bar. iPhones don't let it stay sideways by itself, so upright you'll see the "turn your phone" card.
- **iPhone saves:** the home-screen app has its own games, separate from Safari's. To bring a game across: in Safari, on the title screen, choose the game → Copy to a file; then in the home-screen app, choose an empty game → Load from a file.
- [ ] The icon on the home screen is the monkey on grass under a blue sky, named 小猴子.
- [ ] It opens full screen, sideways, and plays as in the browser.
- [ ] After an upload to GitHub, closing and opening the app again shows the new version (it always loads the newest when there's a connection).
- [ ] With no connection (flight mode), it still opens and plays, voices and all, once it has been opened online just once (wait a minute on that first visit, for the voice clips). On an iPhone, the home-screen app counts as a first visit: open it once online.
- [ ] On a weak signal, it opens from its copy after about 3 seconds instead of waiting, and words are said straight away.
- [ ] Opening the game, you never see it half-built (小猴子 on a black screen, an empty label in the corner and no buttons): until it's ready, the start-up screen shows the app's monkey and three dots, then the console appears all at once, its full size, with the whole title. On a weak signal, after 8 seconds on the start-up screen, it says Slow connection, still loading….
- [ ] In Safari on an iPhone, once you have a game, the title screen says ⛶ Keep your games safe (instead of Play full screen). It opens the steps to add it to the home screen, which say Safari can delete games after a week away, and how to bring a game across (Copy to a file, then Load from a file). Copy to a file works even if you take a while to tap Download.

## Full playthrough of Flower Fruit Mountain (about 20 minutes)

Do this in a **private or incognito window**, so your real save isn't touched. To start again from scratch there: on the title screen, choose an empty game and New game (or delete the game).

**Start**
- [ ] Press Start. The opening plays: the picture fills the screen and the mountain slides to the middle. Each sentence of the story appears as subtitles at the bottom, in Chinese (很久很久以前……, 海里有一座山，叫花果山。 and so on) with pinyin over each word and the English underneath, and is read aloud. 🔊 reads it again and Next moves on; the stone cracks and the monkey is born. Tapping a word shows its pinyin and meaning and says it. Skip is a small button in the top corner (B). With pinyin off in Settings, the pinyin is hidden. The stone, the mountain and the monkey are never hidden by the words. The last caption has Begin. (On a computer, Space or Enter presses Start, Next and Begin.)
- [ ] There's no How to play: a new-word card for 石头 (stone) appears straight away, with the English showing: the picture on the left, the word, pinyin, English and note beside it. Then a message says the old monkey is waiting by your stone.
- [ ] Tips: a yellow bubble over the D-pad says "Hold an arrow to walk. Tap to turn." and goes once you take a step. Facing the old monkey, one over the A button says "Press A to talk"; facing something new, "Press A to look". In the old monkey's "去看看！" line, one points at a grey word: they're just for listening. Once you know 3 words, one points at the Menu for a few seconds. Each tip shows only once.
- [ ] Menu, Settings, How to play has three pages (the controls; green, bold green and grey words, with tapping a word in the example showing its meaning beside it; and the rest), each fitting the screen on a Pixel 8 Pro, even in Chrome; Next, Back and Got it are always in sight at the bottom of the card, even on a small phone.

**The old monkey**
- [ ] Stepping off the stone onto the path is just walking: no word card (路 waits for chapter 1).
- [ ] The rock round the meadow looks like the hillside's cliffs: its top flat grey rock seen from above (a crack or a tuft of grass here and there), and at its front a rock cliff two squares high, lit along its top and darker at its foot, with its shadow on the grass. Where the ground beside the rock is lower, its edge shows, with a little shadow to its right. Your stone is in a nook in it, with the cliff behind it carrying on out of sight at the top of the screen, and a pillar of rock between the nook and the way up, where two boulders sit either side of the big rocks.
- [ ] The old monkey stands just left of your stone, with a ! above him. Walk two steps left (below him), face up and press A.
- [ ] He says 你好！ with 你好 in bold green (its pinyin green too) and the English underneath, then a new-word card for 你好 that stays quiet (he has just said it; 🔊 Hear it says it), then 去看看！ Cards for words you discover yourself, such as looking at a tree, say the word straight away.
- [ ] The task asks you to learn 4 words, with a counter.
- [ ] Learn words from things that sparkle: face a cliff (山), a tree (树) or the river (河) and press A; walk into flowers (花) or tall grass (草). The counter goes up.
- [ ] The sign next to the stone shows 花果山 with its name, and the note "The name of your home." (nothing about a book).
- [ ] Talk to the old monkey again, with 4 or more new words: he says 我们看看！ (Let's have a look!) and you match each word you've learned to its picture, as at the practice post (tap a word, then its picture; a wrong one shakes, and you try again). Not now ends the talk, and he waits until you're ready. Once they're all matched, he says 太好了！ (Great!) and teaches 动物 (animal). A new task asks you to find the hidden animals (0/4). Then, if there are words for the meadow you don't know yet, a message says he wants you to learn them too, naming just those (such as "the river, the fish in the pond and a bird"). TASKS says "Learn the words for the meadow (2/5). Still to find: …", and the list gets shorter as you find them; the checklist has it too. If you already know all five, he doesn't ask.

**The hungry monkey**
- [ ] The monkey to the right of the old monkey has a !. It says 我饿了。我要吃水果！ (I want to eat some fruit: 水果 is grey, just for listening) and teaches 吃 (eat). The task says "Find some fruit for the hungry monkey", with no arrow: finding it is the point.
- [ ] Talk to him again before you've found any fruit: he asks again and says 水果在树上。 (he used to say nothing at all).
- [ ] Peach trees grow at the far left end of the long path; the apple tree is in the south-east, across the river. Face one and press A to learn its word (桃子 peach or 苹果 apple) and pick one.
- [ ] Whichever you bring first, he gives it back: 这是桃子。我要吃苹果！ (or 这是苹果。我要吃桃子！). You reply 对不起 and he says 没关系. The task changes to "Find the apple the hungry monkey wants" (or the peach): plain English while you haven't caught that fruit, and "Find the 苹果 (apple)..." if you have. Once you've picked one, it's "Give the 苹果 (apple) to the hungry monkey".
- [ ] Once he's eaten, he has a ! again. Talk to him: he looks a little embarrassed about being so fussy and says 对不起！ (Sorry!), and you reply 没关系 (that's OK), taught on its own card. The Phrases tab then has 6 of the 7: 不客气 comes in chapter 1, when the big monkey thanks you for the flowers.
- [ ] The thirsty monkey stands near the bottom of the map: when it talks, you both still show above the speech box.
- [ ] Bring the other fruit: 你有苹果吗？ (or 你有桃子吗？). The button says Give it.
- [ ] A new-word card for 要 (want) appears. Then "Fill the gap": 我要吃＿。 with the English underneath, and 3 choices. While the fruit is new they're pictures with the Chinese; once you've recognised it, Chinese and pinyin only. Choosing fills the gap and reads the sentence aloud; a wrong choice shows the right one, and either way the task completes with +3 🐚. The monkey says 谢谢你！ and you don't reply this time: 要 was new, and one new word at a time, so 不客气 waits.

**The big monkey**
- [ ] The big monkey (大猴子) stands in the middle meadow, between the two patches of tall grass on the left, with no !: he has nothing to ask yet (his flowers wait for chapter 1, after the colour lessons). Talk to him: 你好！, and you reply 你好.
- [ ] Nothing in the prologue teaches 红色 (red), 黄色 (yellow) or 白色 (white): walking into the meadow's flowers teaches 花 (flower), and picking them does nothing.

**The thirsty monkey**
- [ ] The thirsty monkey (bottom left) teaches 喝 (drink). A task asks you to collect water.
- [ ] Face the pond (middle of the meadow, where the yellow arrow points). While you don't know 鱼 (fish), A says Look and shows you the pond's fish first ("Little fish dart about in the pond."); then A says Collect, and teaches 水 (water). The river is the same: 河 (river) first, then the water. One new word each time you press A.
- [ ] Take it back. A new-word card for 我 (I) appears, then fill the gap in 我要喝＿。 (I want to drink water.). +3 🐚.
- [ ] The rocks stay until everything else in the meadow is done: the hungry and thirsty monkeys helped, all 4 animals found, and the meadow's five words learned: 山 (mountain), 河 (river), 树 (tree), 鱼 (fish) and 鸟 (bird: now and then a little bird lands on the grass near you, sparkling; face it and press A). With everything else done and a word still to find, the rocks stay and TASKS says which. Then the screen shakes and a message says the rocks north of the meadow have rolled away and the troop gathers there. The old monkey and the little monkey are where the rocks were, with a ! over the old monkey.

**Friends and animals**
- [ ] The little monkey (in the south meadow, on the path near the bottom right, below the 老板's stall) has a ! over it once you've said hello to the old monkey. It teaches 猴子 (monkey) the first time, 朋友 (friend) the second, then offers a word game. The ! goes once it has taught you both words.
- [ ] In the word game, the questions use only words you've learned (pictures and wrong answers too). A question is never about the animal (or monkey) you're playing with. Each right answer fills a red heart; 2 or more makes a friend, and you get a 🐚 per right answer.
- [ ] Once the old monkey has told you about animals, a patch of tall grass rustles now and then: an animal is hiding there. Step onto it and the animal jumps out with a ! over your head. A few steps after meeting one, another hides.
- [ ] South of the meadow (down the path through the gap in the trees), the squirrel sits up a big tree and the goat stands on top of its rock, a cliff in front of it. Face one: A says Meet (for the goat, from the grass just right of it).
- [ ] Little birds land on the grass near you now and then and hop about for most of a minute. Walk up and face one: A says Look and shows 鸟 (bird), then it flies off. Stepping right onto one also makes it fly off.
- [ ] Meeting an animal is a scene down the left of the card, with the question and answers beside it: your monkey, from behind, facing the animal on the grass (the goat on its grey cliff), with the animal's name plate showing 3 hearts and "Question 1 of 3". The animal's own picture is never one of the answers. A right answer makes the animal hop and fills a heart (+♥). At the end: "⭐ New friend" (or "♥ Friends" if it already was) with your hearts and shells, or, with fewer than 2 right, the animal fades away back to its home, in the same layout: the scene down the left (its name plate never covers your monkey), the message in the middle beside it and OK at the bottom right. A battle's end (won, or worn out) looks the same. On an iPhone everything is a little smaller and the answers still fit without scrolling.
- [ ] The first time you meet each animal, a new-word card teaches its name and the encounter doesn't show the English. Meeting it again shows the Chinese name with an English button.
- [ ] Before an animal's questions, it says 我们玩吧！ (Let's play!) in the speech box, its picture and name (狐狸 húli) over the line: the first time, just after its card; after that, with "It wants to play! Answer its questions to make friends." under it (for a friend, "Your 朋友 (friend) wants to play again!"). The button (and A) says Play, and starts its questions.
- [ ] In an animal's question, nothing moves when you answer: the result and Next appear in the bottom row. Right or wrong, the result shows the word with its pinyin and English (Yes! 学校 xuéxiào · school), and you hear it. When listening, each answer shows its pinyin once you've answered. With an apple from the shop, a small hint in the bottom row says "Eat a 苹果 (apple): one wrong answer goes"; tapping it takes one wrong answer away.
- [ ] Animals' questions always have pictures to choose from. A new word is asked by sound ("Listen. Which picture is it?"); after two right answers in a row it's asked by its written form ("Read it."): its characters with pinyin underneath, beside the question and above the answers (never below them), and pictures to choose from, with nothing written under them. On Flower Fruit Mountain you're never asked to pick characters by sound.
- [ ] Two answers never look alike: 家 (home) and 学校 (school), both houses, are never side by side, nor are 床 (bed) and 睡觉 (to sleep), 碗 (bowl) and 吃 (to eat), 水 (water) and 喝 (to drink), 动物 (animal) and an animal, or 猴子 (monkey) and a picture with a monkey in it (such as 老师, the teacher). Matching at the practice post, they're in different rounds.
- [ ] Keep going until you've found all 4 (rabbit and fox in the grass, the squirrel and the goat at their homes).
- [ ] Little birds land on the grass near you; until you know 鸟 (bird), one sparkles. Face it and press A to learn 鸟.

**The training ground**
- [ ] A wooden practice post stands just right of the pond, in the middle of the meadow. Face it: A says Train.
- [ ] Before you have 4 words with pictures, it says to come back later (with a count).
- [ ] After that, it asks if you want to practise and says how many of the words need practice (new ones, or ones you've missed since, until you've got them right twice in a row). Start matches about 8 words to their pictures in 2 rounds. Stop ends it without changing anything.

# Testing Little Monkey

## Automated tests

Run these in the project folder before every commit:

```
npm test
```

They start the game in a pretend browser (no window opens) and check that it loads without errors and shows the title screen, for a new player and for a saved game. They catch typos, missing files and files loaded in the wrong order.

They also check the content files: every word in a sentence is in the word bank, no word is listed twice, every picture is different, characters stand where you can walk, names have Chinese, pinyin and English, the word pictures are complete and up to date, and so on. If a test fails, its message says what's wrong and where.

One test plays Flower Fruit Mountain from a new game to the end of what's built (chapter 1: the school, its lessons and its flowers), pressing the game's own buttons the way a player would (tests/playthrough.test.js). If a change makes the area impossible to finish, it says where it got stuck.

And they try out a few things the game does: the story questions only show words you've caught, it's chapter 1 all the way (no prologue), and Space and Enter work as A on the title screen and in the intro.

They can't tell whether the game *feels* right, so the checks below are still needed.

## Playing on this computer

```
npm start
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in a browser. Press Ctrl+C in the terminal to stop. In Claude Code, the preview pane's "game" setting does the same.

## The screens gallery

With `npm start` running, open http://127.0.0.1:8000/tools/screens.html. It shows every screen of the game side by side at phone size (the title, the opening, tips, How to play, conversations, replies, word cards, fill the gap, the shop, animals, the menu pages, the story and the cave door), each the real game with its own pretend save. Your saved game isn't touched, and it's silent. Change the size to check a small phone or a computer, and "alone" shows one screen bigger. The smallest, Small phone (640 × 360), has its own tighter layout: check that the word test shows both rows, the Word Scroll a whole row of tiles, Settings all its buttons and a battle its answers and the apple without scrolling, a battle where you're hurt and have fruit its three choices (Hit, Eat, Run away) and your health, and the bag its Back. At iPhone (750 × 369) and smaller, every card fits but the Tasks page, the scrolls, How to play and Add to Home Screen, which show ▼ More; no A or B badge covers a letter (OK, Next, Skip, New game, Done, Music); pinyin is never tiny; and in conversations you stay in sight above or below the speech box. To see them as a phone does, open the gallery in a browser at 3× zoom (a phone's pixels): pictures are sized for the screen's real pixels, so a computer's gallery shows some a little bigger or smaller than a phone.

After any change you can see, look through it for things that overlap, buttons you'd have to scroll to find, text that's too long, and screens that don't match the others. A red "Error" under a screen means it didn't load. The screens are listed in tools/screens.js; add one when the game gets a new screen.

## Playing on your phone

Open https://lph147.github.io/little-monkey/ on your phone. GitHub Pages updates it a minute or two after each upload to GitHub. Your phone may keep showing the previous version for up to 10 minutes; if a change hasn't appeared, wait a little and reload.

The save on your phone belongs to this address, so it is separate from saves anywhere else.

### Installing it as an app (full screen)

- **Android (Chrome):** open the address, then menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). Open 小猴子 from the home screen: no address bar, status bar or navigation buttons, and it stays sideways even when the phone is upright. Your save is the same one as in Chrome.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address, then Share → **Add to Home Screen** → Add. Open 小猴子 from the home screen and turn the phone sideways: no Safari bars and no status bar. iPhones don't let it stay sideways by itself, so upright you'll see the "turn your phone" card.
- **iPhone saves:** the home-screen app has its own games, separate from Safari's. To bring a game across: in Safari, on the title screen, choose the game → Copy to a file; then in the home-screen app, choose an empty game → Load from a file.
- [ ] The icon on the home screen is the monkey on grass under a blue sky, named 小猴子.
- [ ] It opens full screen, sideways, and plays as in the browser.
- [ ] After an upload to GitHub, closing and opening the app again shows the new version (it always loads the newest when there's a connection).
- [ ] With no connection (flight mode), it still opens and plays, voices and all, once it has been opened online just once (wait a minute on that first visit, for the voice clips). On an iPhone, the home-screen app counts as a first visit: open it once online.
- [ ] On a weak signal, it opens from its copy after about 3 seconds instead of waiting, and words are said straight away.
- [ ] Opening the game, you never see it half-built (小猴子 on a black screen, an empty label in the corner and no buttons): until it's ready, the start-up screen shows the app's monkey and three dots, then the console appears all at once, its full size, with the whole title. On a weak signal, after 8 seconds on the start-up screen, it says Slow connection, still loading….
- [ ] In Safari on an iPhone, once you have a game, the title screen says ⛶ Keep your games safe (instead of Play full screen). It opens the steps to add it to the home screen, which say Safari can delete games after a week away, and how to bring a game across (Copy to a file, then Load from a file). Copy to a file works even if you take a while to tap Download.

## Full playthrough of Flower Fruit Mountain (about 20 minutes)

Do this in a **private or incognito window**, so your real save isn't touched. To start again from scratch there: on the title screen, choose an empty game and New game (or delete the game).

**Start**
- [ ] Press Start. The opening plays: the picture fills the screen and the mountain slides to the middle. Each sentence of the story appears as subtitles at the bottom, in Chinese (很久很久以前……, 海里有一座山，叫花果山。 and so on) with pinyin over each word and the English underneath, and is read aloud. 🔊 reads it again and Next moves on; the stone cracks and the monkey is born. Tapping a word shows its pinyin and meaning and says it. Skip is a small button in the top corner (B). With pinyin off in Settings, the pinyin is hidden. At 它的眼睛里有光。 two golden beams shoot up from the little monkey's eyes, and the view follows them up into the sky: a red gate with a golden roof stands on the clouds, 南天门 in gold on its blue plaque, the light bursting up through the clouds in front of it; with a little call, two gods slide out from behind its pillars (on the left a red face with horns and a hand held flat over his eyes, on the right a green face with its mouth open and a hand cupped round a big ear). After a couple of seconds the view comes back down to the little monkey as the light fades, then Next brings 你好，小猴子！ The stone, the mountain and the monkey are never hidden by the words. The last caption has Begin. (On a computer, Space or Enter presses Start, Next and Begin.)
- [ ] There's no How to play: a new-word card for 石头 (stone) appears straight away, with the English showing: the picture on the left, the word, pinyin, English and note beside it. Then a message says the old monkey is waiting by your stone.

- [ ] Little birds land on the grass near you now and then and hop about for most of a minute. Walk up and face one: A says Look and shows 鸟 (bird), then it flies off. Stepping right onto one also makes it fly off.
- [ ] Meeting an animal is a scene down the left of the card, with the question and answers beside it: your monkey, from behind, facing the animal on the grass (the goat on its grey cliff), with the animal's name plate showing 3 hearts and "Question 1 of 3". The animal's own picture is never one of the answers. A right answer makes the animal hop and fills a heart (+♥). At the end: "⭐ New friend" (or "♥ Friends" if it already was) with your hearts and shells, or, with fewer than 2 right, the animal fades away back to its home, in the same layout: the scene down the left (its name plate never covers your monkey), the message in the middle beside it and OK at the bottom right. A battle's end (won, or worn out) looks the same. On an iPhone everything is a little smaller and the answers still fit without scrolling.
- [ ] The first time you meet each animal, a new-word card teaches its name and the encounter doesn't show the English. Meeting it again shows the Chinese name with an English button.
- [ ] Before an animal's questions, it says 我们玩吧！ (Let's play!) in the speech box, its picture and name (such as 兔子 tùzi) over the line: the first time, just after its card, and the button (and A) says Play; after that, the line's ▼ is followed by "It wants to play! - [ ] In an animal's question, nothing moves when you answer: the result and Next appear in the bottom row. With an apple from the shop, a small hint in the bottom row says "Eat a 苹果 (apple): one wrong answer goes"; tapping it takes one wrong answer away.
- [ ] Animals' questions always have pictures to choose from. A new word is asked by sound ("Listen. Which picture is it?"); after two right answers in a row it's asked by its written form ("Read it."): its characters with pinyin underneath, beside the question and above the answers (never below them), and pictures to choose from, with nothing written under them. On Flower Fruit Mountain you're never asked to pick characters by sound.
- [ ] Two answers never look alike: 家 (home) and 学校 (school), both houses, are never side by side, nor are 床 (bed) and 睡觉 (to sleep), 碗 (bowl) and 吃 (to eat), 水 (water) and 喝 (to drink), 动物 (animal) and an animal, or 猴子 (monkey) and a picture with a monkey in it (such as 老师, the teacher). Matching at the practice post, they're in different rounds.

**The training ground**
- [ ] A wooden practice post stands just right of the pond, in the middle of the meadow. Face it: A says Train.

- [ ] Once you know 我 (I) and 要 (want), the practice ends with a sentence you know with one of them missing, such as 我＿吃桃子。 with its English, and Chinese and pinyin to choose from. There is no listening round where you pick characters by sound.
